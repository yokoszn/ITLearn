# Getting Started with ITLearn

Welcome to your structured path toward IT mastery. Whether you are new to technology or looking to deepen your expertise, this guide will help you begin.

## Who This Is For

- **Students** preparing for tech careers
- **Career changers** entering IT from other fields
- **IT professionals** looking to fill knowledge gaps or specialise

## Step 1: Pick Your Path

Choose the learning path that best matches your goals and current level.

### For Complete Beginners

- Start with **[Computing Fundamentals](/01%20Computing%20Fundamentals/)**
- Move to **[Networking Fundamentals](/paths/networking)**
- Then explore specialisations

### For Career Switchers

- Begin with **[Linux Systems](/paths/linux)** for hands-on experience
- Add **[Networking Fundamentals](/paths/networking)** for core infrastructure knowledge
- Consider **[Site Reliability Engineering](/paths/sre)** for modern ops practices

### For Current IT Professionals

- **[Site Reliability Engineering](/paths/sre)** for scalable systems design
- **[Cybersecurity Foundations](/06%20Cybersecurity/)** for security-first thinking
- **[Systems Design & Architecture](/07%20Systems%20Design%20&%20Architecture/)** for advanced concepts

> [!NOTE] Learning Philosophy
> Our paths emphasise **understanding over memorisation**. You will build practical skills while learning the underlying principles that make technology work.

## Step 2: Set Your Pace

### Self-Directed Learning

- Each path includes clear milestones and checkpoints
- Progress at your own speed

### Structured Approach

- Dedicate consistent time daily (even 30 minutes helps)
- Complete one milestone before moving to the next
- Practise what you learn with hands-on labs and projects

## Where to Get Help

- **Stuck on a concept?** Check the [Dictionary](/dictionary) for terms and abbreviations
- **Want to practise?** Look for practice labs and environments linked within each path
- **Found an error?** Contribute fixes via [GitHub](https://github.com/yokoszn/ITLearn)

## Your Learning Toolkit

### Essential Tools

Most paths assume you have access to:

- **Computer with internet access** (Linux, macOS, or Windows)
- **Text editor** (VS Code, Vim, or similar)
- **Terminal/command line access**
- **Virtual machine capability** (for lab environments)

### Recommended Resources

- **Practice labs** - Hands-on environments for applying concepts
- **Documentation** - Official docs for tools and technologies
- **Community forums** - Stack Overflow, Reddit, and similar
- **Books and courses** - Supplementary learning materials

## What Makes ITLearn Different

- **Real-world focus** - Learn skills that matter in production environments
- **Vendor independence** - Understand principles, not just specific products
- **Understanding over memorisation** - Build capability, not just certifications

## Ready to Begin?

1. **[Choose your first learning path](/)**
2. **Start learning and building**

> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Don't try to learn everything at once. Pick one path, work through it systematically, and build confidence through practice.
