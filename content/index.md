## Why Digital Sovereignty Matters

We live in an era of increasing digital dependence. Understanding how technology works is not optional. It is essential. Our learning paths build capability. They do not just collect certifications.

**Core principles:**

- **Independence over vendor lock-in**
- **Understanding over memorisation**
- **Practical skills over theoretical knowledge**
- **Community over isolation**
